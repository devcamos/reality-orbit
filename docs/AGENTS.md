# Documentation and Product Scope

## Purpose

This scope governs business requirements, product strategy, research synthesis, design rationale, operating procedures, and repository documentation. It keeps changing business knowledge in Notion while making implementation-relevant decisions discoverable in the repository.

## Authorities

- Start with the linked Notion specification or backlog item.
- Use the [Reality Orbit operating hub](https://app.notion.com/p/3aa7233a96ec810c9de1db94ba8b8796) for product, business, research, finance, and operating knowledge.
- Use the [Reality Orbit Backlog](https://app.notion.com/p/b52c7cc96b124a43a8a1c0606e0cb187) for actionable work and status.

## Rules

- Record the user problem, intended outcome, scope, constraints, acceptance criteria, risks, and evidence for material changes.
- Keep product requirements separate from implementation details.
- Distinguish fact, interpretation, decision, experiment, and open question.
- Do not duplicate a Notion page into the repository unless the repository needs a stable implementation contract.
- Do not expose private feedback, customer data, financial records, credentials, or personal research notes.
- Update documentation when behaviour, public commands, architecture, or operating responsibilities change.

## Review checklist

- Does the change improve understanding rather than add interface noise?
- Does the written hierarchy match the canonical ontology?
- Are acceptance criteria testable?
- Are out-of-scope items and unresolved decisions explicit?
- Is the source of truth linked rather than silently copied?
